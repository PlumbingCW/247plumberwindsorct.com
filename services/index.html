Services that carry emergency intent are the ones where an active problem is common, such as burst pipes, drain and sewer backups, water heater failure, toilet overflows, sump pump failure and boiler no-heat calls. Planned services, such as installations, repiping and camera inspections, are not treated as emergencies, though we answer the phone for them any hour.
