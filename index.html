Windsor, Connecticut

Plumbing in a river town

Windsor sits where the Farmington River meets the Connecticut River, and its villages, Windsor Center, Poquonock, Wilson and Hayden Station, each have their own mix of older and newer homes. The plumbing in them reflects every decade of repairs. That is why an emergency plumber here sees everything from corroded galvanized lines to recent PEX.

Water matters in a town built on rivers. The town's hazard planning documents list Batchelder Road, Pleasant Street and River Street among areas at risk of flooding and describe brooks such as Mill, Meadow and Deckers. If your basement sits near one of those, a working sump pump and a clear drain line are the basics. See sump pump repair and basement waterproofing.

Windsor is an MDC member town, so the MDC supplies water and handles wastewater. The main in the street belongs to the utility, and the pipe on your property belongs to you. We help you work out which side a problem is on, and what to tell the utility. Our office is on the Day Hill Road corridor, which is also home to many businesses that need commercial plumbing help.

Pricing

What affects the price of plumbing work

We do not post dollar figures here because every home is different. What matters is what the job involves. You get a clear price before work starts, and these are the factors behind it.

A pipe under an open basement joist is quicker than one inside a finished wall or under a slab. Access decides how much time and how much repair afterward.

Copper, PEX, galvanized steel and cast iron each repair differently. Old pipe may need more than a spot fix, and we explain when.

After-hours and urgent work involves different scheduling than a planned install. We explain it up front.

A common cartridge costs little. A water heater, a sump pump or a section of sewer pipe costs more, and quality varies. We show options when there are real choices.

Some work does. Rules vary, so ask the Windsor building department what applies. We tell you what to expect.

If a quote is much lower than others, ask what it leaves out. If it is higher, ask what it includes. A clear explanation is part of the service.
