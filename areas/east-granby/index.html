Wells, municipal water and what changes in an emergency

Town planning documents say about thirty percent of East Granby is on the municipal water system, so many homes rely on private wells. State records also list several water utilities for the town, including Old Newgate Ridge, Connecticut Water, Aquarion and the MDC. If you are on a municipal line, a main shutoff and curb valve work like they do elsewhere.

A well changes the emergency steps. If a pipe bursts, shut off the pump breaker as well, so the pump does not keep feeding water into the break. A pressure tank that has lost its air charge makes the pump cycle fast and shortens its life. We can check the tank, the pressure switch and the plumbing on the house side. For pump and water treatment equipment beyond that, we tell you if a well specialist is the right call.

The Farmington River, Salmon Brook and local flooding

The Farmington River forms East Granby's southern boundary, with Salmon Brook and Muddy Brook as its major tributaries and Holcomb, Sanborn and Shelden's Brooks nearby. The town's hazard documents note occasional small stream flooding near Floydville Road and Wolcott Road, and a culvert in the Tunxis and Spoonville area that flooded years ago. The town monitors those spots during heavy rain.

If your property is near a brook, expect more groundwater in wet seasons, and consider a sump pump with a backup. If a wet basement is a repeat problem, basement waterproofing covers interior drainage.

Seasonal pipe problems in rural homes

Rural and semi-rural houses often have long supply runs, outbuildings and exposed pipes in garages and crawl spaces. Cold weather finds those quickly. Insulate exposed pipes, disconnect hoses in the fall and keep heat on in outbuildings with plumbing. If a pipe does freeze, open the faucet, thaw it gently with warm air and call us if it has split.

Water heaters in rural homes work hard, particularly with well water that carries sediment. We do not guess your water quality. A water test tells you what is in it, and we adapt our recommendations to what we find. See water heater repair for the common fixes.

East Granby is home to Old Newgate Prison, a well-known historic site, and the town is more spread out than the center of Windsor. Getting to you can take longer, and we say so honestly. We do not promise arrival times. We answer the phone around the clock and tell you what is possible.

Seasonal checklist for rural homes

Before winter, disconnect hoses and drain outdoor spigots. Insulate pipes in crawl spaces and garages, and check heat tape. Test the well pump and look at the pressure tank gauge. Know where the main shutoff and the pump breaker are.

In spring, check the basement and the well area for signs of flooding or debris. If your well water changes in taste or color after heavy rain, have it tested. A plumber can help with the house side of the system, and a well professional can help with the pump and the well itself.

What to do first in East Granby

Find your main shutoff before you need it. If you are on a private well, the pump may need its own shutoff, so ask the plumber on the call. Do not stand in water near electrical equipment. If you smell gas, leave and call 911. Take photos before cleanup so your insurer can see the damage.
