Three water providers, one town

State records list three water utilities for South Windsor: the MDC, Manchester Water and Connecticut Water. The MDC says it supplies drinking water to portions of South Windsor as a non-member town. Which one serves you depends on your street. Your water bill tells you.

That matters in an emergency. The curb valve and the main in the street belong to your utility, not to you. A plumber works on your side of the meter, and the right utility handles the rest. If you are unsure, call us and we will help you work out who to phone.

Culverts, dams and the summer 2021 storms

South Windsor's hazard planning documents list the Connecticut River on the west, the Scantic River to the north, the Hockanum River to the east and the Podunk River, plus Averys, Bancroft, Dry, Newberry and Waples Brooks. They also record real damage. Tropical Storm Henri washed out a culvert on Chandler Road, and Spring Pond overflowed and damaged a pool. Storms in summer 2021 caused flooding below the Veteran's Park dam off Parkview Drive.

Culverts on Rye Street, Griffin Road, Darthill Road and Strong Road are listed as concerns. If your yard or basement is near any of these, you may see surface water and seepage more often. A sump pump and clear drains are worth the attention.

Single-family homes and their plumbing

South Windsor is largely single-family housing, which means a lot of individual water heaters, sump pumps and sewer laterals, each maintained by its owner. A water heater that is more than ten years old is a candidate for replacement before it fails. A lateral with mature trees nearby is a candidate for a camera inspection.

We keep the work focused on what is actually wrong. If your heater needs a thermostat, we replace the thermostat. If it needs to go, we explain why. For planned work, see water heater installation.

Checking your own property

Walk your lot after a heavy rain and look at where water goes. Note any pooling near the foundation, any culvert or ditch that backs up and any downspout that dumps close to the house. South Windsor's hazard documents point to older culverts on several roads, and private driveways and yards often have their own small ones.

Clear leaves and debris from culvert inlets that you are allowed to reach, and tell the town about public ones. Keeping water moving away from the house reduces the load on your sump pump and your basement.

What to do while you wait in South Windsor

Close the main shutoff and switch off power to wet areas if the breaker panel is dry. Do not walk into standing water in a basement with outlets or a panel. If you smell gas, leave and call 911. Take photos for your insurer, and call the 24/7 line so a plumber can take it from there.
