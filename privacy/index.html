Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we handle when you visit this site or call us.

Calls to our line

The main way to reach us is by phone. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we may ask for your name, address and a description of the problem so we can arrange service. We use that information to respond to your request and to improve our service.

No forms on this site

This website does not have contact forms and does not ask you to submit personal information through the site. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include in the message and use it to reply.

Third-party services

Pages on this site load fonts from Google Fonts and embed maps from Google Maps. Those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser loads them. Their own policies describe how they handle it.

How we use information

We use information you provide to answer your call, schedule and perform plumbing work and keep records of service. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention and your choices

We keep service and call records for as long as needed for the purposes above and to meet legal obligations. To ask a question about your information, call (860) 397-8103 or email contact@247plumberwindsorct.com.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version on this page.
